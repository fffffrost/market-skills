# B2B Announcement Copy

Turn product facts into credible Chinese launch communication without exaggeration or reference-copy imitation.

## Establish the source hierarchy

Use this order when sources conflict:

1. Verified product and team materials.
2. Publicly approved facts and legal wording.
3. Current draft and stakeholder edits.
4. Reference articles or style samples.
5. Desired tone and surface.

Extract the product positioning in one sentence before rewriting. Preserve the product name, company, target customer, scenario, shipped capabilities, availability state, and approved claims.

Read [references/announcement-templates.md](references/announcement-templates.md) for article and repost structures.

## Choose the deliverable

- **Official launch article:** industry change → user problem → product answer → capability proof → customer outcome → availability and CTA.
- **Opening section:** establish the problem and product answer without exhausting the whole story.
- **Repost copy:** one memorable hook, one factual product sentence, and one action.
- **Review:** identify factual risk, overclaim, awkward Chinese, weak proof, repetition, and visible imitation.
- **Version comparison:** recommend one version and explain the decision briefly.

## Draft the narrative

### 1. Lead with the change that matters

Use a concrete market or work change, not empty宏大叙事. Show why the target customer needs a different approach now.

### 2. Make the product role explicit

State what the product is, for whom, and what work it changes. Do not make the reader infer the category from feature paragraphs.

### 3. Turn features into evidence

Connect each capability to a real workflow, mechanism, and outcome. Keep shipped, beta, planned, and aspirational capabilities separate.

### 4. End with a factual next step

State availability, participation conditions, contact or CTA, and any qualification. Avoid ending on an unsupported superlative.

## Style guardrails

- Prefer steady official language over hype.
- Remove unnecessary clauses before adding polish.
- Borrow only high-level structure from references; change the opening rhythm, metaphor, and evidence sequence.
- Avoid absolute language such as “唯一、彻底、全面重构、一个接口全域经营” unless supported and approved.
- For corporate communication, prefer “启动内测、定位于、通过……协同、帮助客户……” over inflated certainty.
- Keep sentences easy to quote and repost.

## Output order

Return the usable copy first. Then provide a short editorial note covering what changed, what was softened, and which claims still need confirmation.

Do not use this skill for consumer ad slogans, fictional launches, or a general brand strategy task.
