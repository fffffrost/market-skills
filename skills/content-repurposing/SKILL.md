# Content Repurposing

Create new channel experiences from one source of truth instead of shortening the same copy repeatedly.

## Lock the source of truth

Extract and label:

- facts, numbers, names, dates, quotes, and approved claims;
- central argument and supporting evidence;
- target audience and intended action;
- reusable stories, examples, visuals, and sound bites;
- statements that require context or must not be separated.

Do not introduce new claims during adaptation. Flag missing facts instead of filling them creatively.

Read [references/channel-patterns.md](references/channel-patterns.md) when producing more than two channel variants.

## Design the content package

### 1. Define a job for each channel

Assign one primary job: attract attention, explain, prove, invite participation, convert, nurture, or enable sales. Match the audience state and CTA to that job.

### 2. Select a channel-native angle

Choose a different useful entry point for each asset: surprising finding, common mistake, practical checklist, customer story, behind-the-scenes process, executive viewpoint, or direct offer.

### 3. Rebuild the structure

Do not preserve the source outline by default. Use the native rhythm of the target surface:

- WeChat repost: context, takeaway, reason to read, link action.
- Xiaohongshu-style note: concrete hook, structured value, lived detail, lightweight CTA.
- LinkedIn: professional tension, point of view, evidence, question or action.
- Short video script: spoken hook, one idea per beat, visual cue, closing action.
- Sales enablement: customer situation, proof, objection response, next step.

### 4. Preserve traceability

Maintain a source map for every fact and quote. Mark any interpretive headline or shortened quote that needs approval.

## Output order

1. Content package overview with channel, audience, job, angle, and CTA.
2. Publishable assets, clearly separated by channel.
3. Source-to-asset traceability notes.
4. Approval or production dependencies.

## Quality gates

- Each variant can stand alone for its channel audience.
- Variants are not cosmetic length changes.
- Facts and tone remain consistent with the source.
- The CTA matches the channel job and audience readiness.
- The package avoids repeated hooks competing on the same day.

Do not use this skill to invent a content strategy without a source asset or factual base.
