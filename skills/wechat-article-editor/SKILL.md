# WeChat Article Editor

Edit for reader progression, credibility, and publication readiness rather than decorating every sentence.

## Confirm the local publishing context

Record the account identity, follower relationship, target reader, content objective, approved terminology, CTA destination, and review owner. If the user needs current platform limits or operational publishing rules, verify them from current first-party documentation instead of relying on memory.

Keep editorial quality separate from platform operation. This skill prepares copy and review notes; it does not access the account, choose distribution, or guarantee reach.

## Confirm the editing mode

- **Diagnostic review:** identify structural, factual, tonal, and readability problems without rewriting everything.
- **Structural edit:** reorder sections, remove repetition, repair logic, and rewrite transitions.
- **Line edit:** improve sentence clarity, rhythm, terminology, and consistency.
- **Publication pass:** check title, abstract, subheads, CTA, image notes, links, and final errors.
- **Version comparison:** recommend one base version, then merge only demonstrably stronger elements.

Clarify target reader, account identity, communication objective, intended action, factual source hierarchy, length limit, and non-negotiable wording.

Read [references/wechat-edit-checklist.md](references/wechat-edit-checklist.md) for a formal publication pass.

## Edit in layers

### 1. State the article promise

Write one sentence answering: after reading, what will the reader understand, believe, or do? Remove or move sections that do not serve this promise.

### 2. Repair the reading path

Use a coherent progression such as change → problem → explanation → evidence → implication → action. The exact structure may vary, but every section must earn the next one.

### 3. Strengthen evidence

Separate fact, company claim, customer statement, interpretation, and aspiration. Flag unsupported numbers, absolute statements, and future capabilities described as current.

### 4. Edit for Chinese rhythm

Prefer concrete subjects and verbs. Break overlong sentences, remove duplicated modifiers, reduce “通过……从而……” chains, and avoid consecutive abstract nouns. Preserve useful industry terminology.

### 5. Build the publishing package

Provide one recommended title, a short abstract, scannable subheads, cover text if needed, and a clear final CTA. Offer alternatives only when they represent different reader hooks.

## Output order

1. Publishable revised article or the requested edited section.
2. Recommended title and abstract.
3. Concise change log grouped by structure, evidence, and language.
4. Facts or approvals still required.

For a diagnostic-only request, lead with the five highest-impact issues and quote the affected passage briefly.

## Guardrails

- Do not silently alter facts, numbers, names, quoted speech, or product state.
- Do not flatten a distinctive author voice into generic corporate language.
- Do not create clickbait that the article cannot fulfill.
- Do not produce ten equivalent title options.
- Do not use this skill for short social captions or pure ad copy.
